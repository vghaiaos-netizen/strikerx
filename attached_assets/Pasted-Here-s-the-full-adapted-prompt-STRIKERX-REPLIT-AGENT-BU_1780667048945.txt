```
Player selects deposit currency
        ↓
CryptoBot generates invoice for that currency
        ↓
Player pays
        ↓

        ↓
System fetches live exchange rate for that currency to TON
        ↓
Amount converted to TON equivalent
        ↓
TON equivalent × 100 = STRIKER credited to player
        ↓
Balance updated, GroupBot announces if above threshold
```

Withdrawal flow:
```
Player requests withdrawal specifying amount and destination
        ↓
Wagering requirement check
        ↓
STRIKER ÷ 110 = TON amount
        ↓
10% spread captured automatically
        ↓
CryptoBot sends TON to player wallet
        ↓
Transaction logged, GroupBot announces
```

Minimum deposit: 0.5 TON equivalent. Minimum withdrawal: 1000 STRIKER. New account first withdrawal held for manual review.

**STEP 8 — THE FOUR GAMES**

**The Shot — Crash Game**
Multiplier starts at 1.00x and rises continuously. Player bets STRIKER and watches it climb. Cash out at any point to lock in multiplier × bet. If they hold past the crash point they lose their bet. Crash point calculated server-side with provably seeded RNG with 4% house edge baked into distribution. Multiple players in same round visible to each other for social proof. Auto-cashout setting available. Visual: animated football flying toward goal, crowd noise rising with multiplier, ball hitting post on crash.

**Penalty — Binary Bet**
Player picks left, center, or right. Goalkeeper dives to random position. Correct prediction pays 1.92x (4% house edge). Wrong prediction loses stake. Instant result, high frequency. Visual: penalty spot, goalkeeper dive animation, crowd reaction.

**Minefield — Mines Game**
Football pitch grid, configurable 3×3 to 7×7. Player selects difficulty (sets number of hidden defenders). Pick squares one at a time — safe squares increase multiplier, hitting a defender loses entire bet. Cash out after any safe square. Multiplier math accounts for 4% house edge across probability. Visual: pitch grid, defender reveal animation, always-visible cash out button.

**Free Kick — Plinko Game**
Ball drops from top through rows of defender pegs bouncing randomly left or right. Lands in multiplier slot. Slots range 0.2x to 100x, distribution weighted for 4% house edge. Player selects bet amount and risk level (low/medium/high changes slot distribution). Visual: football pitch view, ball physics animation, crowd reaction on big multipliers.

**Golden Boot — Jackpot Pool**
1% of every bet across all four games feeds the pool continuously. Pool displayed live in Mini App and announced in group every 30 minutes. Trigger is random, weighted toward higher bettors. Minimum pool to trigger: 50 TON. On trigger: 90% to winner, 10% retained as revenue. New pool seeds immediately at 10 TON. Winner announced explosively in group. Pool always appears imminent visually regardless of actual amount.

**The League — Tournament System**
Daily tournaments reset midnight. Weekly tournaments reset Monday. Leaderboard ranks by highest single-session multiplier. Prize pool funded by 10% rake on tournament bets plus optional BOOT token entry fee. Auto-announces start, hourly updates, results, and payouts. Flash tournaments launchable anytime from admin dashboard. World Cup edition tournaments during June 11 to July 19 with themed prizes.
